Technology

Cynosure Vectus™ + Skintel®

We invested in medical-grade laser technology that reads melanin levels and adjusts in real time. That means safer, more effective hair removal for all skin tones — including melanin-rich skin that many clinics can't treat properly.

First-time clients get a free consultation and $50 off a full-body laser session.
